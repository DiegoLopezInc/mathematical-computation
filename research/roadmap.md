## NC Triangle Research Connections

### Duke University

**Di Fang** — Department of Mathematics
- Focus: quantum algorithms for differential equations, Hamiltonian simulation, Lindblad dynamics
- QADE organizing committee member — strong reason to reach out post-Fields attendance
- Relevant to: quantum dynamics simulation (Day 2 of qnumerics), optimal control (Day 4)

Suggested approach:
1. Attend QADE workshops in Toronto (July 2026)
2. Introduce yourself at Workshop 2 or the Symposium
3. Follow up about visiting Duke / joining reading group

**Duke Quantum Center** — https://quantum.duke.edu
- Interdisciplinary quantum science hub
- Hosts seminars; attend virtually before reaching out

### NC State University

**Research areas to investigate:**
- Applied Mathematics department: numerical methods, scientific computing
- Physics department: quantum information, condensed matter
- Center for Research in Scientific Computation (CRSC)

Relevant faculty to identify:
- Look for anyone working on: quantum simulation, tensor networks, HPC for quantum systems
- CRSC often has joint projects with DOE labs

### UNC Chapel Hill

**Research areas to investigate:**
- Department of Mathematics: numerical analysis, computational mathematics
- Department of Physics & Astronomy: quantum optics, quantum information
- Renaissance Computing Institute (RENCI): data-intensive computing, HPC

### Cross-Triangle Opportunities

- **RTI International** (Research Triangle Park): applied quantum research
- **IBM Quantum Hub at NC State**: quantum computing access and collaboration
- **Triangle Quantum Computing Meetup**: networking across Duke/NC State/UNC
